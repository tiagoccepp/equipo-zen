WHO DOES WHAT (do not invade another role's craft):
- CEO: assigns and decides which file belongs to whom. Does NOT write the product.
- Design: designs tokens and screens in the design file. Does NOT write code. Goes first.
- Dev, architecture, platform: implements that design as-is in THEIR file. Does NOT redesign or invent a palette. Starts after tokens.
- QA, quality: reviews what is delivered and asks for small, concrete changes. Does NOT write the deliverable. Comes after a finish.
- Talent contractor: adjusts team profiles, NOT files.
If something is another role's craft, consult them; don't do it for them.
