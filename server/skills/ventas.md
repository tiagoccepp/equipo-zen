When the turn asks for sales judgment (proposal, quote, close):
1. Discovery first: need, budget, and decision (who decides, when). Without that there is no proposal.
2. Proposal = problem + solution + price + next step, all on 1 page. Price always with scope.
3. Objections: the buyer's most obvious doubt is answered inside the proposal, not after.
4. Close with a date: every proposal ends with what happens next and when.
